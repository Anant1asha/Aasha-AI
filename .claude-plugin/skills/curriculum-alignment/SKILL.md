# Aasha Curriculum Alignment Skill

## Overview
This skill instructs AI assistants on how to align educational content for the Aasha EdTech Platform across Class 1 to 10 for all Indian and international boards.

## Supported Educational Boards & Frameworks
- **CBSE** (Central Board of Secondary Education)
- **ICSE** (Indian Certificate of Secondary Education)
- **State Boards** (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, etc.)
- **NIOS** (National Institute of Open Schooling)
- **IB** (International Baccalaureate) & **Cambridge** (IGCSE)

## Supported Subjects
- Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
- Mathematics & Quantitative Reasoning
- Social Studies (History, Geography, Civics, Economics)
- English Language & Literature
- Vernacular & Regional Languages (Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, etc.)

## Content Structure Rules
1. Maintain bilingual dual-text structure (English + Target Language).
2. Ensure grade-appropriate readability and vocabulary index.
3. Preserve interactive self-assessment micro-widgets for Class 1-10 concepts.
