# Authority Chain

1. School-book-derived content is the educational foundation.
2. Question Bank supplies assessment inputs.
3. Games and simulations produce interaction evidence only.
4. Assessment converts responses into evidence.
5. TEAS deterministically computes mastery and progression signals.
6. Reward Engine grants rewards only from eligible verified events.
7. Impact Engine aggregates evidence into reporting metrics.
8. AI may propose transformations, explanations, questions, or asset matches, but never overrides the above authorities.
