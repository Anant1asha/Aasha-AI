Node 1: The Four-Chambered Human Heart (मानव हृदय के चार कोष्ठ)

The human heart has four chambers to prevent the mixing of oxygenated blood and deoxygenated blood: the upper thin-walled chambers are the left atrium and right atrium, while the lower thick-walled muscular chambers that pump blood out of the heart are the left ventricle and right ventricle.

Node 3: Arteries, Veins, and Capillaries (धमनी, शिरा और केशिका)

An artery carries blood away from the heart under high pressure and therefore has thick, elastic walls. A vein collects blood from different organs and brings it back to the heart with one-way valves to prevent backflow. A capillary has a single-cell thick wall allowing exchange of oxygen, nutrients, and carbon dioxide with body tissues during double circulation.
