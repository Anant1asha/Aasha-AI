Node 1: Monomial, Binomial, Trinomial & Polynomial Expressions (एकपदी, द्विपदी, त्रिपदी और बहुपद)

An algebraic expression containing one term is a monomial (like 5x), two terms is a binomial (like a + b), and three terms is a trinomial (like a² + 2ab + b²). In general, an expression with one or more terms with non-negative integral exponents is called a polynomial.

Node 3: Standard Identities and Factorisation (मानक सर्वसमिकाएँ और गुणनखंडन)

An equality true for every value of its variables is called a standard algebraic identity: (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b², (a - b)² = a² - 2ab + b², and a² - b² = (a + b)(a - b). Writing a polynomial as the product of irreducible factors is called factorisation.
