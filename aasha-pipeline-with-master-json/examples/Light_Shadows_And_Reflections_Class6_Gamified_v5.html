Node 1: Transparent, Opaque and Translucent Objects (पारदर्शी, अपारदर्शी तथा पारभासी वस्तुएँ)

Objects like the Sun that give out or emit light of their own are called luminous objects. If we cannot see through an object at all, it is an opaque object. If you are able to see clearly through an object, it is allowing light to pass through it and is transparent. Objects through which we can see, but not very clearly, are known as translucent.

Node 3: Pinhole Camera & Mirrors and Reflections (सूचीछिद्र कैमरा तथा दर्पण एवं परावर्तन)

Light travels in a straight line. Because light travels in straight lines, a pinhole camera forms an inverted (upside-down) image on its tracing paper screen. When light falls on a shiny surface like a plane mirror, the mirror changes the direction of light that falls on it — this is called reflection of light.
