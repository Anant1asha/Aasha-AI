Node 1: Standard Form ax + by + c = 0 (मानक रूप)

An equation of the form ax + by + c = 0, where a, b, and c are real numbers such that a and b are not both zero, is called a linear equation in two variables (x and y). Unlike a linear equation in one variable which has a unique solution, a linear equation in two variables has infinitely many solutions.

Node 3: Geometric Representation on Coordinate Axes (निर्देशांक अक्षों पर ज्यामितीय निरूपण)

The graph of every linear equation in two variables is a straight line. Every point (x, y) on the line satisfies the equation, and every solution of the equation is a point on the line. The equation of the x-axis is y = 0, and the equation of the y-axis is x = 0. Any line of the form y = kx always passes through the origin (0, 0).
