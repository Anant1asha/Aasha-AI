Node 1: Do Liquids Conduct Electricity? (क्या द्रव विद्युत का चालन करते हैं?)

Materials that allow electric current to pass through them easily are good conductors of electricity, whereas materials that do not allow current to pass easily are insulators. Acidic, basic, and salt solutions act as an electrolyte and conduct electric current. Distilled water is free of salts and is a poor conductor unless salt or acid is dissolved in it.

Node 3: Electroplating in Daily Life (विद्युत लेपन के उपयोग)

The process of depositing a layer of any desired metal on another material by means of electricity is called electroplating. When electric current is passed through a copper sulphate solution, copper dissociates into free copper and gets deposited on the electrode connected to the negative terminal of the battery.
