Node 1: Subatomic Particles and the Atomic Nucleus (अवपरमाणुक कण और नाभिक)

An atom consists of a dense positively charged central nucleus containing the proton (+1 charge) and neutral neutron (0 charge), while negatively charged electrons revolve in discrete Bohr energy shells or orbital paths (K, L, M, N). The maximum number of electrons in a shell of principal quantum number n is given by the Bohr-Bury rule 2n² (K=2, L=8, M=18, N=32).

Node 3: Valency, Isotopes, and Isobars (संयोजकता, समस्थानिक और समभारिक)

The combining capacity of an atom determined by electrons in its outermost shell (octet rule = 8) is called its valency. Atoms of the same element having the same atomic number (proton count) but different mass numbers (different neutron count, such as Carbon-12 and Carbon-14) are each called an isotope. Atoms of different elements with different atomic numbers but the same mass number (such as Calcium-40 and Argon-40) are each called an isobar.
