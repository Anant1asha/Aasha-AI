Node 1: Four Factors of Production (उत्पादन के चार कारक)

Every production activity in Village Palampur combines four essential factors: 1) Land (measured in hectare units) and natural water for irrigation, 2) Labour (skilled and manual workers), 3) Physical Capital — divided into fixed capital (tractors, turbines, buildings used for many years) and working capital (HYV seeds, fertilisers, and cash in hand used up during production), and 4) Human Capital (knowledge and enterprise).

Node 3: Multiple Cropping and Farm Surplus (बहुविध फसल प्रणाली और अधिशेष)

To grow more than one crop on a piece of land during the year is known as multiple cropping. With electric tube-well irrigation and HYV seeds, wheat yield per hectare rose from 1300 kg to 3200 kg, allowing medium and large farmers to sell their surplus wheat in the Raiganj market and accumulate working capital for the next season.
