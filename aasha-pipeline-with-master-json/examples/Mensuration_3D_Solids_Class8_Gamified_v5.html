Node 1: Surface Area of Cube, Cuboid and Cylinder (घन, घनाभ और बेलन का पृष्ठीय क्षेत्रफल)

A cube has 6 equal square faces of side a, so its total surface area is 6a² and its lateral surface area (4 walls) is 4a². A cuboid of length l, breadth b, and height h has total surface area 2(lb + bh + hl). A right circular cylinder of radius r and height h has curved (lateral) surface area 2πrh and total surface area 2πr(r + h).

Node 3: Volume and Capacity (आयतन और धारिता)

The amount of space occupied by a three-dimensional object is called its volume. For a cube of side a, Volume = a³. For a cuboid, Volume = l × b × h. For a cylinder of base radius r and height h, Volume = πr²h (where 1000 cm³ = 1 Litre).
