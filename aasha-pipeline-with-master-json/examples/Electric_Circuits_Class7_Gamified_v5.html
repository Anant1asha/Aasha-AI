Node 1: Symbols of Electric Components (विद्युत अवयवों के प्रतीक)

An electric circuit connects a cell, a bulb, and a switch using conducting wires. When the switch is in the ON (चालू) position, the circuit is closed and current flows from the positive terminal to the negative terminal.

Node 3: Heating & Magnetic Effects of Electric Current (तापीय और चुंबकीय प्रभाव)

When electric current passes through a high-resistance nichrome wire, it produces heat (Joule heating: H = I²Rt). An electric fuse is a safety device that melts and breaks the circuit if current exceeds a safe limit. Hans Christian Oersted discovered that a current-carrying wire also deflects a magnetic compass needle, forming an electromagnet.
