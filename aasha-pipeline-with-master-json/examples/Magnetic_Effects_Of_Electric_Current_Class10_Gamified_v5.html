Node 1: Oersted's Experiment, Magnetic Field Lines & Right-Hand Thumb Rule (ओर्स्टेड का प्रयोग और दक्षिण-हस्त अंगुष्ठ नियम)

When electric current passes through a metallic conductor, a magnetic compass needle placed nearby shows a deflection, proving that an electric current produces a magnetic field. Magnetic field lines emerge from the North pole and merge at the South pole outside the magnet, and never intersect each other.

Node 3: Solenoid, Electromagnet, Armature & Galvanometer (परिनालिका, विद्युत-चुंबक और गैल्वेनोमीटर)

A coil of many circular turns of insulated copper wire wrapped closely in the shape of a cylinder is called a solenoid. Placing a soft iron core inside a current-carrying solenoid forms a strong electromagnet. In an electric motor, a rectangular armature coil rotates using a split-ring commutator, whereas a galvanometer detects the presence and direction of induced current in a circuit.
