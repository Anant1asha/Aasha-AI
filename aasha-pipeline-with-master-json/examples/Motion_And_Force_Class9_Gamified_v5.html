Node 1: First Law of Motion & Inertia (गति का प्रथम नियम और जड़त्व)

An object remains in a state of rest or of uniform motion in a straight line unless compelled to change that state by an applied unbalanced force. The natural tendency of objects to resist a change in their state of rest or motion is called inertia. The mass of an object is a measure of its inertia.

Node 3: Momentum & Third Law of Motion (संवेग तथा गति का तृतीय नियम)

The momentum (p) of an object is defined as the product of its mass (m) and velocity (v): p = m × v. According to the Third Law of Motion, to every action there is an equal and opposite reaction, acting on two different bodies.
