Node 1: Acids and Bases in Everyday Life (दैनिक जीवन में अम्ल और क्षारक)

Curd, lemon juice, orange juice, and vinegar taste sour because they contain an acid. The chemical nature of such substances is acidic. Baking soda tastes bitter and feels soapy to touch because it is a base. Special substances called an indicator change their colour when added to an acidic or a basic solution.

Node 3: Neutralisation Reaction (उदासीनीकरण अभिक्रिया)

The reaction between an acid and a base is known as neutralisation. Salt and water are produced in this process with the evolution of heat: Acid + Base → Salt + Water + Heat (अम्ल + क्षारक → लवण + जल + ऊष्मा). For example, hydrochloric acid (HCl) reacts with sodium hydroxide (NaOH) to form sodium chloride (NaCl) salt and water.
