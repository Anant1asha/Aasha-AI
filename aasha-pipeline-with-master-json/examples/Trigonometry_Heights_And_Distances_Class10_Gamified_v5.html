Node 1: Right-Triangle Trigonometric Ratios (समकोण त्रिभुज के त्रिकोणमितीय अनुपात)

In a right-angled triangle for acute angle θ, the longest side opposite the right angle is the hypotenuse, the side opposite to θ is the perpendicular, and the base next to θ is the adjacent side. The primary ratios are: sine (sin θ) = perpendicular / hypotenuse, cosine (cos θ) = adjacent / hypotenuse, and tangent (tan θ) = perpendicular / adjacent.

Node 3: Angle of Elevation and Angle of Depression (उन्नयन कोण और अवनमन कोण)

The line of sight connects the observer's eye to the object. When the object is above the horizontal level, the angle between the line of sight and the horizontal is the angle of elevation. When the observer looks down at an object below the horizontal level, the angle formed is the angle of depression, which equals the alternate interior angle of elevation.
