Node 1: Contact and Non-Contact Forces (संपर्क तथा असंपर्क बल)

A push or a pull on an object is called a force, characterized by both its magnitude and direction. Forces that act only through physical contact include muscular force and friction. Non-contact forces act across a distance without physical contact, including magnetic force, electrostatic force, and gravitational force.

Node 2: Interactive Pressure P = F/A & Liquid Column Manometer (AVR-P315)

Node 3: Fluid Pressure and Atmospheric Pressure (द्रवों तथा वायुमंडलीय दाब)

The force acting on a unit area of a surface is called pressure (Pressure = Force / Area, measured in Pascals or N/m²). Liquids exert pressure on the walls of their container that increases with depth, measured by a U-tube manometer. The envelope of air around the Earth exerts atmospheric pressure on all surfaces.
