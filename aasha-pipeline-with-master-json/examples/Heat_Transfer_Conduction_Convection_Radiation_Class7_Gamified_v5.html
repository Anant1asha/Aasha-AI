Node 1: Measuring Temperature — Clinical & Laboratory Thermometers (तापमान मापन — डॉक्टरी और प्रयोगशाला तापमापी)

Temperature is a reliable measure of the hotness of an object, measured using a thermometer on the celsius scale (°C). A clinical thermometer reads human body temperature from 35°C to 42°C (normal human body temperature is 37°C), whereas a laboratory thermometer ranges from -10°C to 110°C.

Node 3: Conduction, Convection, Sea Breeze, and Radiation (चालन, संवहन, समुद्री समीर और विकिरण)

In solids, heat is transferred from the hotter end to the colder end by conduction. In liquids and gases, heated fluid rises and cooler fluid moves in by convection, creating daytime sea breeze and nighttime land breeze in coastal areas. Heat from the Sun reaches the Earth without requiring any medium by radiation.
