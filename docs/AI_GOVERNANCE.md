# Aasha AI Governance — Beta

## AI may assist

- textbook structure extraction
- OCR cleanup
- learning-node drafting
- bilingual wording suggestions
- question drafting
- misconception suggestions
- visual/asset discovery
- teacher/admin summaries
- content QA assistance

## AI may not be authoritative

- final content publication
- final mastery determination
- progression decisions
- reward authorization
- manual evidence verification
- privacy/consent policy decisions
- impact claims without traceable evidence

AI educational output follows: AI draft → schema validation → rule checks → human validation → versioned production content.

The Beta runtime contains no uncontrolled child-facing AI chat.
