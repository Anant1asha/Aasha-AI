🧬 Foundations of Mendelian Inheritance

1. Heredity (आनुवंशिकता): The transmission of genetically determined characters and traits from parents to offspring across successive generations.

2. Allele (युग्मविकल्पी): One of two or more alternative versions of a gene that arise by mutation and are located at the same relative place on a chromosome.

3. Genotype vs. Phenotype: The genotype is the internal genetic constitution (e.g. TT, Tt, tt), whereas the phenotype is the observable physical appearance (e.g. Tall plant vs. Dwarf plant).

4. Monohybrid Cross: A breeding experiment between two individuals focusing on the inheritance of a single pair of contrasting characteristics (such as plant height).

5. Law of Segregation: The two alleles of each gene separate during gamete formation so that each egg or sperm cell carries only one allele for each inherited trait.
